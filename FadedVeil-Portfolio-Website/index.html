ABOUT ME

FadedVeil

A quiet soul with a chaotic side. 🖤 Gamer, creator, esports enthusiast, and someone who finds comfort in the darker, more mysterious side of things.
I am passionate about gaming, especially VALORANT and competitive esports, while also enjoying the creative side of the community — from designing graphics and building Discord spaces to helping teams and organisations grow

I’m naturally intuitive, caring, and observant. I may not always be the loudest person in the room, but I notice more than I say. I value genuine connections, loyalty, communication, and people who make others feel safe enough to be themselves.
Between late-night games, purple aesthetics, gothic vibes, questionable amounts of chaos, and trying to turn ideas into something real, I’m always working on becoming a better version of myself.
Quiet energy. Dark aesthetic. Big heart. Endless ambition. 🖤💜
